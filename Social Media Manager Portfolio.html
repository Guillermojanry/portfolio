From random posting to a plan

The client’s dilemma

  • They didn’t know where to start.
  • They had a lot of footage but no idea how to turn it into posts.
  • They didn’t know what to post, so every post felt like a guess.
  • They posted when they had time, not on a plan.
  • Posts went up with no clear next step, so people watched and moved on.
  • Nothing they posted led to messages, and they couldn’t tell why.

Current situation

  1. Posting random content.
  2. Posting content because the owner wanted to post it.
  3. Posting content without a strategy.
  4. A profile that didn’t say what the business offers or how to get in touch.
  5. Posting at times chosen by habit, not by when the audience was online.

The goal

  1. Get more inquiries.
  2. Increase brand awareness.
  3. Have a clear strategy.
  4. Give every post a clear next step.
  5. Know which posts lead to messages.

What I did

  1. Audit. Reviewed the last 30 posts, the profile, and the audience to see where the account stood.
  2. Research. Studied similar accounts and what their audiences were asking.
  3. Strategy. Chose the platform, the content types, and a goal for the month.
  4. Plan. Built a four-week calendar where every post has a job.
  5. Approve. Sent the plan for written approval before making anything.
  6. Create. Wrote captions and made the videos and graphics.
  7. Publish. Checked every post, then scheduled it.
  8. Engage daily. Replied to comments and messages in set daily windows.
  9. Report. Tracked the numbers weekly and sent a monthly report.
  10. Optimize. Changed one thing at a time and kept what worked.

The strategy

  1. Account audit. Start from what the account already does well and where it falls short.
  2. Content strategy. Decide what to post, with a clear job for each type of post.
  3. Engagement strategy. Plan daily time to reply, join conversations, and pass leads on.
  4. Profile fix. Make the bio, link, and buttons say what the business offers and how to get in touch.
  5. A next step on every post. Give each post one clear action, and track which ones lead to messages.

The challenges

  1. Trial and error with the content. Changed one thing at a time and judged each type of post over 3 to 5 posts before deciding.
  2. Low engagement at first. Started conversations by commenting on other accounts while the audience grew.
  3. Keeping approvals on schedule. Set a reply deadline, followed up before it ran out, and agreed what happens if there’s no answer.
  4. Working with the footage on hand. Told the client exactly what to film, vertical with clear audio, and planned posts around what they had.
  5. Keeping a steady posting rhythm. Lowered the frequency to what could be kept up every week.
